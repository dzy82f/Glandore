<h4>Julian Macnamara</h4>
	
	<p>
	Julian’s background spans consulting, leadership and organisational design, with a long career helping companies and institutions see the hidden forces shaping their world. What he enjoys most is building ideas with others — especially when that stretches the boundary of what an organisation can be. His work blends rigour, curiosity and practicality, with a lifelong fascination for how real systems behave. He builds things by exploring them: conversations, prototypes, frameworks and epistemic engines. What begins as a question often becomes a method; what begins as a pattern becomes a structure. He treats thinking as a discipline, uses conversation as a working tool, and approaches complexity as something to be worked with, not avoided.
	</p>
	
	<p>
	He is the founder of Glandore and the creator of Tychevia® — a dialogue-driven approach to understanding complexity. Tychevia is derived from Tyche – the Greek personification of chance, fortune, and the unpredictable currents of life – combined with via, Latin for way or path. Together, Tychevia means “a way through chance”: a deliberate path with uncertainty rather than against it.
	</p>
	
	<p>
	Glandore is deliberately different. It is an experiment in building a community of practice — a network of people, methods, artefacts, dialogue and digital associates rather than a traditional firm. Its work happens through structured conversations, evolving frameworks and persistent epistemic systems that learn over time. Glandore.com is the public expression of that practice — a living laboratory for complex adaptive systems, human judgement and digital associates working together.
	</p>
	
	<p>
	Tychevia® is the clearest example: an evolving collaborative system built not by instruction or hierarchy, but through dialogue, reflection and shared discovery. It embodies the belief that meaning is co-created, that good systems are always learning, and that exploration itself can be a disciplined form of work. It signals a framework that treats complexity, randomness, and emergence not as noise to be eliminated, but as the very medium through which meaning, strategy, and stewardship are co-created.
	</p>
